How I work

Sales and delivery in one pair of hands

I lead an engagement from first contact through proposal, budget and risk pricing to handover. I keep track of its economics and margin throughout, not just at signing.

Scope grounded in research and data

I decide on priorities and scope with product owners and client management. I rely on user research and data: only what has been validated gets developed.

Relationships for years, not one project

Long-term clients generated about two thirds of the agency's revenue. Cooperation grows from trust: personal care for the relationship and reliable delivery on commitments.
